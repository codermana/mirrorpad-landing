Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mirrorpad. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Mirrorpad is designed from the ground up to be a privacy-first application.

2. Data Collection & Usage

Mirrorpad operates entirely on your local area network (LAN). We do not collect, store, or transmit your drawing data, strokes, screen contents, or any personal information to any cloud servers.

  • Local Streaming: All communication between your drawing tablet and your computer occurs locally over your own Wi-Fi or wired network.
  • No Tracking: We do not include any analytics trackers, diagnostic reporters, or third-party tracking scripts within the mirroring application.
  • No Cloud Accounts: You are not required to create an account or sign in to use Mirrorpad.

3. Security

You can configure local security settings, such as a connection password, within the Mirrorpad desktop application to ensure only authorized devices on your network can connect to your canvas.

4. Website Analytics

This website may collect basic, anonymous usage data (such as page views) to help us improve our site. This is completely separate from the Mirrorpad desktop and browser application.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Mirrorpad's practices, please contact CoderMana Technologies.
